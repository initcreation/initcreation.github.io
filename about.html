Privacy

Why there are no names or photos here

We share names and backgrounds with clients on the introductory call, and in every proposal and contract. We keep them off the public web for the same reason we advise clients to limit what is public about their staff: it reduces phishing and impersonation risk.

Limits

What we don't do

Saying no clearly is part of doing the work well.

  1. No penetration tests or audits

    Our security work is practical basics: checks with established free tools, careful setup and hardening, and monitoring. For penetration tests, audits and compliance work we refer you to specialist firms.

  2. No checks without written permission

    We only check or change a website, domain or account after you confirm the scope in writing.

  3. No vague pricing

    Every engagement starts with a written proposal: deliverables, timeline and a fixed price.
