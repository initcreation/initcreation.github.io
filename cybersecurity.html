Deliverables

How the work is delivered

Every engagement is agreed in writing and ends with something your team can use.

  1. Written permission and scope first

    You confirm in writing which domain, website or accounts are included before we check or change anything.

  2. A plain-language report or a completed setup checklist

    Checks end in a short report with prioritised fixes. Setup work ends with a checklist of what was configured and where.

  3. Step-by-step fixes, or we implement them

    Your team can follow our instructions, or we make the changes for you.

  4. A re-check

    Once the fixes are in place, we check again and confirm what has been resolved.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this a penetration test?

No. The website security check is a basic security check, not a penetration test. We look at configuration and at what is publicly visible, using established free tools and a manual review. We do not try to break into your systems.

Do you ever test without permission?

Never. A website security check only starts after you give written permission for a defined scope. The free public exposure snapshot uses public data only; nothing is scanned or tested.

What tools do you use?

Established free tools such as Mozilla HTTP Observatory and testssl.sh, standard DNS and email record checks, certificate transparency logs and ReconDB data, followed by a manual review. We follow published guidance such as the OWASP and CIS recommendations.

What if we need a penetration test or a compliance audit?

For penetration tests, compliance audits or incident response, we recommend a specialist security firm. We're happy to help you prepare, for example by getting the basics in place first.

How long does it take?

The free snapshot usually takes two to three days. For a website security check, you receive the report within a week, and we re-check within 30 days of your fixes. Setup work depends on how many domains, websites and accounts are involved, and we confirm the timeline in writing before we start.
