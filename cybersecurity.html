Services

What we offer

Penetration testing

Manual testing of web applications, APIs and mobile apps, following the OWASP testing guides.

  • Evidence and fix guidance for each finding
  • Retest after remediation

Attack surface monitoring

Ongoing discovery and monitoring of your internet-facing assets, using ReconDB data.

  • Subdomains, open services and technologies
  • Alerts for new exposure and expiring certificates
  • Monthly summary for management

Cloud and CI/CD hardening

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you ever test without permission?

No. Active testing only takes place under a signed scope and authorisation. The free exposure report uses public information only.

Which standards do you follow?

OWASP WSTG, ASVS and the API Security Top 10 for testing, and CIS benchmarks and ISO 27001 controls for hardening.

Can you help with regulatory requirements?

Yes. We provide testing evidence and remediation support for requirements such as Nepal Rastra Bank IT guidelines, ISO 27001 and customer security questionnaires.

How long does a penetration test take?

A typical web application test takes one to two weeks, depending on its size.
