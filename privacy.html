Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Data collection

We collect only the information needed to respond to you and provide our services. This includes:

  • Details you submit through our contact form: name, email address, company or website, the service you are interested in, budget range and your message.
  • Technical logs related to website performance and security.

2. Use of information

Your information is used only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about our services.
  • Improve our security intelligence and the ReconDB platform.

3. Data protection

We use industry-standard encryption and access controls to protect your data. We never sell your data to third parties.

4. Third-party services

We use AWS (Amazon Web Services) to process form submissions and deliver email. These services comply with major international security standards.

Our contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to protect against spam and automated abuse. Turnstile processes limited technical information about your browser and connection to confirm that a person is submitting the form. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

5. Contact

For questions about this policy or to request deletion of your data, email mail@initcreation.com.np.
