FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

A company website usually takes two to four weeks. Stores and custom applications depend on scope, and the proposal includes a timeline.

Do you use WordPress?

When it suits the project, yes, configured and maintained securely. For many projects we recommend lighter, lower-maintenance alternatives.

Can you redesign our current website?

Yes. We review the existing site for speed, search and security, then redesign it while protecting your search rankings.

Do you integrate Nepali payment gateways?

Yes: eSewa, Khalti and Fonepay, as well as Stripe and PayPal for international customers.

Planning a new website or application?

Tell us what you have in mind and we will reply within one business day.
