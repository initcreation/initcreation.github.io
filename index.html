Our approach

Why clients work with us

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is included in the free exposure report?

A short summary of what your organisation exposes to the internet: subdomains, publicly reachable services, outdated software and signs of leaked data. It is built from public sources only, and we never scan or test your systems without written authorisation.

Do you work with clients outside Nepal?

Yes. Most of our work is delivered remotely, and we keep overlapping hours with Europe, the Gulf, Asia-Pacific and the US.

How do you price projects?

Most projects are fixed-price, based on a written scope agreed before work begins. Monitoring, maintenance and support are available as monthly plans.

How do you handle our data in AI projects?

We limit what data the system can access, use providers that do not train on your data, test for prompt injection and data leakage, and can deploy on infrastructure you control.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. We provide security testing, AI features and development for web and marketing agencies, under your brand and under NDA if needed.
