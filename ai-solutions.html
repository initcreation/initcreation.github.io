Approach

How we build reliable AI

Most AI projects fail quietly: they look good in a demo and drift in production. We make quality visible from the first week.

  1. Define success

    Agree the metrics that matter for your use case, and the threshold for going live.

  2. Measure a baseline

    Build a test set from your real data and measure where you are today.

  3. Build

    Choose the simplest approach that can reach the target, with validation on every output.

  4. Evaluate and stress-test

    Test accuracy, edge cases and misuse, including a security review of the AI feature.

  5. Deploy with monitoring

    Launch with logging, quality monitoring and human review for uncertain cases.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will our data be used to train public models?

No. We use providers and settings that do not train on customer data, and we can deploy fully private setups on your infrastructure. Your data is used only for your project and deleted at the end unless you ask us to keep it.

Which models and tools do you use?

We choose per project: established commercial models, or open-source models such as YOLO, PaddleOCR and Qwen-VL when data must stay in-house or costs must stay low. We recommend the simplest option that meets the target.

How do you measure whether the AI works?

Before building, we agree the metrics that matter for your use case, such as field accuracy, groundedness or false accept rate, and measure a baseline. Every pilot ends with a results report against those metrics.

How much data do we need?

Often less than expected. Many pilots start with a few hundred representative documents or images. We will tell you early if the data is not enough to reach the target.

Can you work with Japanese documents and content?

Yes. Our AI lead has delivered Japanese OCR, speech recognition and customer support projects. Communication in Japanese depends on the project; ask us.

Can it run on our own servers or devices?

Yes. We can deploy in your cloud account, on premises or on edge devices, and optimise models to run without a GPU where possible.

Have a process AI could take over?

Tell us about it. We will reply within one business day with questions and a suggested pilot.
