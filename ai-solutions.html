The problem

Where AI usually helps first

Repeated questions

Customers and staff ask the same questions every day, and answering them takes skilled people away from other work.

Information that is hard to find

Policies, procedures and past answers are spread across files, email and chat, so people ask colleagues instead.

Manual document handling

Forms, invoices and reports are read and retyped by hand, which is slow and introduces errors.

Services

What we offer

Support and sales assistants

Assistants for your website, WhatsApp or Messenger that answer in English and Nepali and hand over to a person when needed.

  • Trained on your FAQs, policies and products
  • Lead capture and hand-off to your CRM
  • Conversation logs and human escalation

Internal knowledge search

Ask questions across your documents and get answers with links to the source.

  • Works with Google Drive, Notion, Confluence and PDFs
  • Respects existing access permissions
  • Answers cite their sources

Document and workflow automation

Extract, classify and route information from documents and email without manual retyping.

  • Invoices, forms, applications and reports
  • Integration with your existing systems
  • Time saved is measured, not estimated

AI security testing

An independent review of AI features you already run or plan to launch.

  • Prompt injection and jailbreak testing
  • Data leakage and access control review
  • AI usage policy and readiness review
Deliverables

What you receive

Every engagement is scoped in writing and ends with something your team can use.

  1. Agreed goals

    A short workshop to choose the use case and define how success will be measured.

  2. Working pilot

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which AI models do you use?

We choose for each use case: established commercial models, or open-source models hosted on your own infrastructure when data has to stay in-house.

Can the assistant understand Nepali?

Yes. We build and test for English, Nepali and Romanised Nepali.

How long does a pilot take?

Usually two to four weeks from kickoff to a working system with measured results.

Will our data be used to train public models?

No. We use providers and settings that do not train on customer data, and we can deploy fully private setups.

Not sure where AI fits in your business?

In a 30-minute call we can identify the most promising use case and what a pilot would involve.
