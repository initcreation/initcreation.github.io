What we build

Four areas where our AI lead has hands-on experience. Each starts with a small, fixed-price pilot, proof of concept or review.

Document AI and data extraction

Turn scanned reports, forms, invoices and other documents into validated, structured data your systems can use.

  • Scanned reports, forms and other semi-structured documents
  • OCR combined with small language models fine-tuned using LoRA
  • Schema validation and a confidence score on every field
  • Uncertain fields sent to a person for review
  • Accuracy measured on your own documents

First step: Pilot on your documents, 2–4 weeks

Edge computer vision and visual inspection

Detect, track and measure objects in live video, fast enough to run on site or on small devices.

  • Real-time detection, tracking and measurement
  • FP16/INT8 quantisation with TensorRT and ONNX Runtime
  • Under 60 ms per inference reached on constrained hardware; we measure on yours
  • Data pipelines that keep results reliable as conditions change
  • Runs in the cloud, on site or on device

First step: Proof of concept, 4–6 weeks

AI agents, assistants and evaluation

Agents and assistants that work from your documents and tools, with quality and security measured before launch.

  • Agent workflows with explicit steps and tool calls; a person approves risky actions such as payments or deletions
  • Support and knowledge assistants grounded in your documents, with hand-off to a person
  • Quality measured with RAGAS and automated judge tests: grounding, relevance, tool-call reliability
  • Checked for prompt injection, data leakage and tool misuse
  • Review of an AI feature you already run

First step: Pilot or review, 1–4 weeks

Robotics simulation and synthetic data

Generate synthetic training data and test robot and vision models in simulation, before investing in physical hardware.

  • Simulation environments in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab
  • Synthetic images for training inspection models
  • Benchmarks of vision-language-action models for robotic grasping
  • Automated data generation that runs without physical hardware

First step: Feasibility study, 1–2 weeks (selected projects only)

Also on request: private LLM fine-tuning for sensitive data.

Approach

How we build reliable AI

Most AI projects fail quietly: they look good in a demo and drift in production. We make quality visible from the first week.

  1. Define success

    Agree the metrics that matter for your use case, and the threshold for going live.

  2. Measure a baseline

    Build a test set from your real data and measure where you are today.

  3. Build

    Choose the simplest approach that can reach the target, with validation on every output.

  4. Evaluate and stress-test

    Test accuracy, edge cases and misuse, including a security review of the AI feature.

  5. Deploy with monitoring

    Launch with logging, quality monitoring and human review for uncertain cases.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will our data be used to train public models?

No. We use providers and settings that do not train on customer data, and we can deploy fully private setups on your infrastructure. Your data is used only for your project and deleted at the end unless you ask us to keep it.

Which models and tools do you use?

We choose per project: established commercial models, or open-source models such as YOLO, PaddleOCR and Qwen-VL when data must stay in-house or costs must stay low. We recommend the simplest option that meets the target.

How do you measure whether the AI works?

Before building, we agree the metrics that matter for your use case, such as field accuracy, groundedness, detection accuracy or latency, and measure a baseline. Every pilot ends with a results report against those metrics.

How much data do we need?

Often less than expected. Many pilots start with a few hundred representative documents or images. We will tell you early if the data is not enough to reach the target.

Can it run on our own servers or devices?

Yes. We can deploy in your cloud account, on premises or on edge devices, and optimise models to run without a GPU where possible.

Have a process AI could take over?

Tell us about it. We will reply within one business day with questions and a suggested pilot.
